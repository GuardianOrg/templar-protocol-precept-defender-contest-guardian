## Tradeoff assessment

The strongest counterargument is to wait for the registry-native successor: it avoids two immutable Blend-core forks, duplicate liquidity, a security-critical migrator, weekly upstream review, bridge custody, and an indefinite permissionless-user tail. ADR 0008’s schedule-abandonment gate is therefore essential, not administrative overhead.

The real tension is minimal executable delta versus minimal lifetime system burden. Six uniform ABI-boundary traps are slightly larger than trapping only the state-creation roots, but are the smaller coherent security boundary: every stock effectful emissions export becomes deterministically inert, while removing modules/hooks would widen deposit/withdrawal fund-path review. ABI compatibility is preserved at the wire/spec level, but callers intentionally receive `BadRequest` instead of stock authorization/state-dependent outcomes.

## Security and maintenance assessment

The six traps themselves are coherent and complete for fresh constructor state. Stock `distribute`, `gulp_emissions`, `add_reward`, `remove_reward`, `claim`, and `drop` cover the public roots that write reward/distribution/backfill/accrual state, create allowances, authorize the backstop, invoke Comet, mint LP-backed shares, or call the emitter (`vendor/blend-contracts-v2/backstop/src/contract.rs:258-310`; `vendor/blend-contracts-v2/backstop/src/emissions/manager.rs:17-55,94-200,206-297`; `vendor/blend-contracts-v2/backstop/src/emissions/claim.rs:12-90`). The seven-day upstream/advisory checkpoint is burdensome but has a concrete owner, fail-closed consequence, and no silent forward-port path.

## Blockers

1. **ADR 0008 still treats the selected emissions profile as unresolved.** ADR 0008 says emissions remain a separate undecided gate and that ADR 0011 “must choose” the profile (`0008:19-21`, `0008:184-198`), while ADR 0011 requires ADR 0008 to select this exact trapping-backstop profile and contain no unresolved disposition (`0011:188-193`). This also leaves ADR 0008’s declared two-file bundle inconsistent with ADR 0011’s required three-file bundle (`0008:93-96`, `0011:213-214`). **Minimum correction:** make ADR 0008 select ADR 0011’s exact forked-backstop profile, state that the factory is byte-identical and the backstop is forked, replace provisional emissions language with the six-trap invariant, remove obsolete stock-emissions canary implications, and declare ADR 0008/0009/0011 as the reviewed bundle.

2. **Successor replacement has two incompatible lifecycle models.** The operative lifecycle is a lane within one literal legacy lineage (`0009:194-222`), and replacement is described as revising that same lineage while retaining the predecessor route until its authorization horizon drains (`0009:424-429`). Acceptance instead requires a “fresh lineage” (`0009:580-587`) even though the lineage key is the immutable legacy-pool address and the record may name only one current successor (`0009:113-116`). A literal implementation can therefore either create a second lineage/T0 or reject the intended replacement. **Minimum correction:** require each replacement to use a fresh lane within the existing lineage, preserve the lineage’s one T0, and define the predecessor/current-successor route transition after predecessor authorization and reservation drain.

3. **The bridge ceiling equation contradicts replenishable capacity.** The normative ledger counts `settled advances + live reserved advances` against the ceiling (`0009:349-355`), while every successful transaction atomically restores the bridge and reconciles its reservation (`0009:215-217`, `0009:371-387`), and acceptance requires capacity to replenish after reconciliation (`0009:588-590`). Read literally, cumulative settled volume permanently consumes the ceiling and eventually halts all migrations. **Minimum correction:** define the counted quantity as live reservations plus any included-but-not-yet-reconciled bridge deficit, remove it only after transaction and live-balance proof of atomic restoration, and state that reconciled settled volume remains in the conservation ledger but not current capacity exposure. If a cumulative lifetime cap is intended instead, remove the replenishment claim and size that cap explicitly.

## Consequence simulations

1. **Comet/backstop getter failure:** stock `set_status` calls `backstop.pool_data` before dispatch (`pool/src/pool/status.rs:10-18`), and `pool_data` calls Comet supply/balance getters (`backstop/src/backstop/pool.rs:23-40`). ADR 0008’s early authenticated status-4 branch (`0008:137-139`, `0008:492-493`) therefore gives the intended getter-independent freeze. If a separate pool-local failure prevents that call, the policy’s all-reserves-disabled fallback remains one-way; no reactivation path appears.
2. **Permissionless emissions calls:** the six public effectful exports route to all reward-zone, distribution, backfill, accrual, user-emission, allowance, token, Comet, emitter, and receiver roots (`backstop/src/contract.rs:180-306`; `backstop/src/emissions/manager.rs:17-253`; `backstop/src/emissions/distributor.rs:11-145`; `backstop/src/emissions/claim.rs:12-70`). An unconditional first-operation `BadRequest` in all six closes those roots. The retained deposit/withdraw hooks only read absent emission data, pool `gulp_emissions` rolls back on the trapped cross-contract call, and the deployed admin tree cannot authorize pool emissions configuration.
3. **Foreign LP participation:** public deposit can create user/pool shares and later q4w state; no trapped emissions root is needed. The foreign-state tombstone preserves the exact owner, amount, and unlock state rather than erasing the claim (`0008:395-400`, `0009:499-500`). **NON-BLOCKING clarification:** because the byte-identical factory permits any self-authorized admin to deploy another pool (`pool-factory/src/pool_factory.rs:61-72,98-120`) and the backstop accepts every factory-recognized pool (`backstop/src/backstop/pool.rs:93-99`), “no two pools share” should ideally say “no two official ADR-0008 cohorts share”; ADR 0008 already excludes other factory deployments from official identity (`0008:307-308`).
4. **Failed migration canary:** an unknown or failed attempt remains `CANARY_PENDING`; sequence consumption and timeout do not release it. Only non-inclusion or included atomic rollback, expiry of every bound, state reconciliation, and a fresh reviewed authorization return the lane to `DEPLOYED` (`0009:202-213`). This prevents duplicate movement. Blocker 3 must be resolved so the capacity release rule is equally deterministic.
5. **Successor replacement:** retaining the incumbent route until its authorization and reservation horizon drains prevents an old signed transaction from landing against a retired route. The next lane may then canary the replacement and switch one current route. The safety flow is defensible, but blocker 2 must make clear that this is a lane replacement inside the existing lineage, not a fresh lineage.

## Alternative fairness

The alternatives are materially fair. ADR 0008 distinguishes no launch, a real funded backstop, shared shim, repairable proxy, governance control, and a narrowly isolated immutable shim. ADR 0011 compares retained stock emissions, emitter-only denial, ABI deletion, no-backstop redesign, and body-level trapping against the stated drivers. ADR 0009 separately rejects manual migration, privileged transfer, universal atomicity, default incentive, liability transfer, mutable migrator, donation-based vault headroom, and timer-based retirement. The chosen designs are not made to win by omitting their principal cost: two maintained forks, indefinite tail support, funded migration liquidity, and immutable containment rather than patching.

## Security, maintenance, and verification assessment

The authority model is strong: the policy has a narrow immutable surface; status 4 is absorbing; bridge source-account authorization is bound to one frozen transaction; user authorization, reservations, route phase, and live state all fail closed; sink ownership is terminal. Maintenance claims are honest about immutable fork ownership, weekly upstream/advisory review, route closure on evidence gaps, reproducible Wasm, and migration-only recovery. Verification is unusually concrete across effect-root tests, authorization trees, exact rounding vectors, crash/retry behavior, testnet/mainnet canaries, live readbacks, and terminal reconciliation. The three blockers are correctable specification inconsistencies; they do not require rejecting the underlying decision.
