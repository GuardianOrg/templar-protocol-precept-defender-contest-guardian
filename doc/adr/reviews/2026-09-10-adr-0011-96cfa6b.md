## Tradeoff assessment

- **Strongest counterargument to the selected option:** skip ADR 0008 entirely and wait for the registry-native profile (`0011:169-171`; ADR 0009 alternative at `0009:551-552`). The bundle commits a small team to two immutable Blend-core forks (pool seven restrictions, backstop six traps), a security-critical migrator, bridge multisig custody with fresh-key attestations, a seven-day upstream checkpoint that gates routing (`0011:134-144`, `0008:612`), and an indefinite permissionless tail, all under a 100,000 USD envelope (`0008:108-115`). The ADRs concede this and keep the abandonment gate; the tradeoff is stated honestly rather than hidden.
- **Real tension in ADR 0011:** trapping six public roots at the ABI boundary (rather than emitter-only denial or deleting `emissions/**`) buys the smallest fund-path diff and an equal generated spec, at the cost of retaining dead-but-compiled emissions modules and internal `update_emissions` hooks whose inertness is a genesis-reachability argument (`0011:89-95`), not a structural absence. The ADR converts that into a testable obligation (acceptance 6–8) and is right that rewriting deposit/withdraw would widen the audit surface.
- **Real tension in the corrected ADR 0009 text:** a faulty successor stays “sole current successor” with its route closed until every reservation and authorization horizon drains before a replacement may open (`0009:127-130,461-464`). Recovery is slower during I1, in exchange for no still-valid signed envelope landing against a retired route. Defensible.
- **Composition:** the coupling reason (`0008:94-98`) is concrete: launch safety claims depend on the exact backstop profile and the exact exit path. ADR 0011 acceptance 1–2 are satisfied by the ADR 0008/0009 blobs at this candidate (ADR 0009 retains only pool b-token emissions absence from ADR 0008’s orphan handling, foreign-share/q4w terminal branches, and no-incentives text; grep for `tombstone|allowance` finds only migrator allowance at `0009:305,609`).
